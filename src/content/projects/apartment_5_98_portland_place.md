body: |
  In the open-plan living, dining and kitchen space, the grey wallpaper gives way to a warm polished plaster that catches the light from the chandelier and the tall sash windows. In the guest en suite, the bath is replaced by a walk-in shower in book-matched marble with brushed brass fittings.

  The wider scheme includes refinished oak floors, new kitchen doors that keep the original mirrored glass, dark walnut panelling in the principal bathroom, a lighter wallcovering on the study balcony and the principal bedroom terrace brought inside as part of the room.

  We also designed the digital sales brochure, an interactive page-turning edition styled to suit the apartment.
