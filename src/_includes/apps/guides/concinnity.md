concinnity helps you collect tasks, decide what matters today, make time for the work, and see how long it actually takes. You can use it on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Getting Started

The app has four main places:

- **Today** shows your day’s timeline, planned tasks, and calendar events.
- **Tasks** holds everything you need to do, including tasks you have not planned yet.
- **Projects** groups related tasks and shows their progress.
- **Insights** shows completed work, focus time, and patterns.

Start by adding a task in **Tasks**, then plan time for it in **Today**. You can add details whenever you need them.

## Adding a Task

1. Open **Tasks** and type a name in **Add a task**.
2. Press **Return**. A name is all you need to get started.
3. Open the task to add optional details: notes, subtasks, priority, an estimate, a deadline, a project, an area, tags, or a repeat schedule.

Tasks appear under **To Plan**, **Planned**, **Deferred**, or **Completed**. Tap a task’s completion circle when it is done. You can reopen a completed task from its details.

## Planning Your Day

For a guided plan, open **Today** and choose **Plan the Day**. The steps help you review calendar events, unfinished work and deadlines; choose tasks and your **Top 3**; estimate the work; and check whether it fits. Nothing is saved until you confirm the plan.

To plan one task directly:

1. Open **Today** and choose **Plan a Task**. On iPhone, tap **+**; touch and hold it for more options.
2. Choose an existing task or enter a new one.
3. Choose the day, start time, and length, or mark it **All Day**.
4. Tap **Schedule**.

A task is the work you need to do. Its planned time is a place for that work on your timeline. You can give one task more than one planned time. Removing it from the timeline keeps the task in your list.

## Changing Plans

Tap planned time on the **Today** timeline to change its day or time, start focusing, or open the task’s details. You can also open a task and choose **Replan**. When you need another work session for the same task, choose **Add another planned time**.

Use **Replan Unfinished** on Today to deal with work left over from earlier plans. Use **Shut Down the Day** to review what happened and decide what to carry forward. Tap the date on Today to open the month, or open the week view to look further ahead.

## Priorities and Projects

Set your **Top 3** on Today to keep the day’s most important tasks visible. Completing one there completes the same task everywhere in the app.

In **Projects**, choose **New Project** to group related tasks. Open a project to add tasks and see its progress. For other ways to organise work, create **Areas** and **Tags** in **Settings**. You can assign them from a task’s details.

## Focus and Time Tracking

Open a task or its planned time and tap **Focus**. Choose a timer, then work while concinnity tracks the session. You can pause, add time, or finish. When finishing, choose whether to complete the task too.

Focused time is separate from estimated and planned time. **Insights** uses your completed tasks and focus sessions to show your progress. You can change Pomodoro and Deep Focus intervals in **Settings → Focus**.

## Calendar and Reminders

Allow calendar access to see your events beside planned tasks on **Today**. In **Settings → Calendars**, choose which calendars appear. Calendar events remain in your calendar; viewing them does not turn them into tasks.

In **Settings → Reminders**, you can turn on alerts for the start of planned time, upcoming deadlines, and the end of focus time. Allow notifications when the app asks. Reminders are delivered on the device where you set them up.

## Sync and Privacy

Turn on **Sync with iCloud** in **Settings** and sign in to the same Apple Account on each device. Tasks, projects, and plans can then sync through your private iCloud account. Changes may take a little time to appear elsewhere.

Your work stays on the device too, so you can keep using the app offline. Calendar events stay in your calendar rather than being copied into the planner.

## Troubleshooting

**A task has disappeared from To Plan.**  
Check the **Planned**, **Deferred**, and **Completed** sections in **Tasks**. You can also use **Search**.

**My tasks are not showing on another device.**  
Check **Settings → iCloud** on both devices, make sure sync is on, and confirm both use the same Apple Account. Reopen the app after changing the sync setting.

**Calendar events are missing.**  
Check calendar permission and the calendars selected in **Settings → Calendars**. The app can only show calendars available on that device.

**I am not getting reminders.**  
Check that the reminder is on in **Settings → Reminders** and that notifications are allowed for concinnity in your device’s system settings.
