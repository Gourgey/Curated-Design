The Byron Penthouse

Stunning, dual aspect 3 bedroom penthouse overlooking Regent's Park
The Residence

Quiet grandeur, moments from Regent's Park

Set on one of London's most elegant addresses, The Byron Penthouse pairs generous volume with a calm, considered interior. Beneath dramatic pitched ceilings and tall arched windows, warm polished plaster and light oak herringbone parquet run throughout, with bespoke joinery and brass detailing giving every space a sense of occasion.
