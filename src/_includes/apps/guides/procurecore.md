ProcureCore keeps your studio's projects, products, suppliers and orders in one place, on iPhone,
iPad and Mac. This guide covers the essentials.

## 1. Getting started

1. **Create an account.** Sign up with your email and a password, or use **Sign in with Apple**.
2. **Choose how to work.** Tap **Continue as a solo designer** to start your own workspace, or
   enter an **invite code** from a colleague to join their studio.
3. **Follow the checklist.** The **Dashboard** shows a short Getting Started list: create a
   project, import a product, place it in a room and export a client-ready PDF.

**Finding your way around.** On Mac and iPad, use the sidebar. On iPhone, the tabs at the bottom
show Dashboard, Projects, Board and Products. Suppliers, Search, Data Transfer, Recently Deleted
and Settings are under **More**.

## 2. How your work is organised

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Project** | One client job, such as "Kensington Flat". |
| **Style** | A design option within a project, such as "Modern heritage". You can create several styles to compare options. |
| **Area** | A room or space in the project, such as "Reception" or "Main bedroom". |
| **Product** | An item in your studio's **Product Library**. You add it once and can use it in any project. |
| **Placement** | A product placed in a specific project, style and area, with its quantity, pricing and order status. |
| **Supplier** | A company you buy from, with contacts, account number, payment terms and lead times. |

## 3. Projects

- **Create a project:** go to **Projects** and tap **Add Project**. Add the client, address,
  budget, dates and at least one style.
- **Add areas:** open the project and tap **Edit Areas**.
- **Track phases:** add phases (for example "Concept Design") with dates and a status.
- **Templates:** to reuse a project's setup, choose **Save as Template**. Start the next project
  with **New Project from Template**.
- **Duplicate:** choose **Duplicate…** and pick what to copy across. Order, payment and delivery
  statuses always reset on the copy.
- **Archive:** use the filter at the top of Projects to switch between Active, Archived and
  Templates.

## 4. Adding products

You can add a product in three ways:

- **From a link (fastest):** tap **Add Product from Link** and paste the product page's web
  address. ProcureCore reads the page and fills in the name, price, dimensions, images and
  supplier. Check the details, then tap **Save Product Only** or **Save & Place**. Some websites
  block automatic reading. In that case, use **Open in Browser & Capture**, wait for the page to
  load (complete any "verify you are human" step), then tap **Capture Page**.
- **Manually:** tap **Add Product** and fill in the fields. **Auto-fill from text** can pull
  details out of a pasted description.
- **From another project:** in a project, choose **Select Existing Product** to use something
  already in your library.

If you add a product that is already in your library, ProcureCore asks whether to merge, replace
or keep both. To refresh a product's price and details from its website later, open it and use
**Update from Source**.

## 5. Placing products and tracking orders

- **Place a product:** open the product or project and tap **Add Placement**. Choose the project,
  style and area, then set the quantity and pricing. A **Missing info** flag shows which details
  still need filling in.
- **Procurement Board:** shows every placement across all projects. Group and sort it, then use
  **Mark Approved**, **Mark Ordered**, **Mark Paid** and **Mark Delivered** as each item moves
  along.
- **Request quotes:** select placements on the Board and tap **Request Quote**. ProcureCore drafts
  one email per supplier for you to review and send.
- **Reprice:** if a product's price changes, use **Reprice…** to update its placements. Locked
  placements (already ordered or signed off) are left alone.
- **Client sign-off:** use **Record Client Sign-off** to log the client's approval of selected
  items, with an optional drawn signature.
- **Attachments and comments:** add spec sheets, quotes or notes to products, projects and
  placements.

## 6. Budgets and currency

- Each project shows **Estimated** and **Committed** spend against its budget, broken down by
  style and area.
- Set your studio's currency and measurement units in **Settings**.
- If a product is priced in a different currency, it is flagged. Open it and use **Convert to [your currency]** to record a
  converted price. ProcureCore never converts prices automatically, and the original supplier
  price is always kept.

## 7. Exporting

Open a project or style and tap **Export**:

- **PDF (client-ready):** a presentation with your studio name and logo. Choose which styles to
  include.
- **Excel (.xlsx):** each style on its own tab.
- **CSV:** a single sheet for older tools.

Add your studio name and logo in **Settings** so they appear on PDF covers.

## 8. Working as a team

**Invite someone:** go to **Settings → Manage Team → Invite a teammate**, choose a role, and share
the code. Each code works for one person and expires after 14 days. The new teammate enters the
code when they sign in (or under **Settings → Join another studio** if they already have an
account).

**Roles:**

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including managing the team, billing and deleting projects. |
| **Admin** | Manage the team and all project data, including deleting projects. |
| **Member** | Create and edit project data. Can't manage the team or delete projects. |
| **Viewer** | See everything, but can't make changes. |

Owners, Admins and Members are **editors** and count towards your plan's editor limit. Viewers
are unlimited. An unused Admin or Member invite holds an editor place until it is used, cancelled
or expires.

Everyone in the studio sees the same projects, and changes sync to all devices automatically.

## 9. Plans

| | **Free** | **Studio+** | **Studio Pro** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Projects | 3 | 20 | Unlimited |
| Products | 30 | 350 | Unlimited |
| Editors | 1 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Read-only viewers | — | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Studio name on exports | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| AI assistant | — | — | ✓ (100 messages a month) |

Current prices are shown in the app. Plans are available monthly or annually, and Studio Pro
includes a **7-day free trial** with 10 AI messages.

- **Upgrade:** tap any upgrade prompt, or open **Settings**. Only the studio owner can subscribe,
  and the subscription covers the whole team.
- **Switch between monthly and annual, or cancel:** use **Manage Subscriptions** in the app, or
  your Apple Account's subscription settings. Changes take effect at your next renewal.
- **Restore:** if you reinstall or change device, use **Restore Purchases**.
- **If you downgrade:** when you have more editors than the new plan allows, the most recently
  joined editors become viewers. Nothing is deleted.
- **If a subscription ends:** the studio returns to Free and everyone except the owner becomes a
  viewer. Your data stays, and editing returns when you resubscribe.

## 10. The AI assistant (Studio Pro)

Tap **Ask AI** and ask questions about your studio in plain English, for example "Which projects
are over budget?" or "What's overdue for delivery?"

- **Scope:** ask about your whole studio, or pick a single project from the scope menu. If your
  studio is very large, you'll be asked to choose a project. Nothing is sent and no message is
  used when that happens.
- **Allowance:** 100 answered messages per subscription month (10 during the trial). Unused
  messages don't roll over. Studios with a lot of data may reach the monthly usage limit sooner.
- **Privacy:** client names, notes and tracking numbers are left out unless you turn on
  **Include client & notes**. Your chat history is stored only on your device. **Clear Chat**
  removes it.
- The assistant answers questions. It does not change your data.

## 11. Sharing data and backups

**Data Transfer** lets you send projects, products or suppliers to another ProcureCore user as a
`.procurecore` file.

- **Share Everything**, or **Choose What to Share…**. Picking a project automatically includes its
  styles, areas, placements and the products and suppliers it uses.
- To receive data, choose **Open a .procurecore File…**, review what's inside and choose how to
  handle duplicates.
- These files are not encrypted and can include client details, so only share them with people
  who should see that information.

## 12. Deleting and restoring

Deleted projects, styles, products and suppliers go to **Recently Deleted**, where you can
**Restore** them. Only owners and admins can permanently delete projects.

## 13. Sync and offline use

- ProcureCore works offline. Your changes are saved on your device and sync when you reconnect.
- To sync straight away, use **Sync now**.
- If the same item is changed on two devices at once, a **sync conflict** appears on the
  Dashboard. Open it and choose **Keep Mine** or **Keep Theirs**.

## 14. Troubleshooting

**My subscription isn't showing.**
Open the upgrade screen and tap **Check Subscription Status** or **Restore Purchases**. Make sure
you're signed in to the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

**It says this Apple Account already has a subscription.**
The subscription may belong to another ProcureCore studio. Tap **Refresh App Store Status**. Use
**Restore Purchases to This Studio** only if you want to move paid access here. Doing so removes
it from the other studio.

**My changes aren't syncing.**
Check your internet connection and tap **Sync now**. If a "Sync problem" message appears, tap
**Try Again**. As a last resort, **Reset Local Data & Re-pull** downloads a fresh copy from the
server. Export a backup first if you have changes that never synced.

**I see duplicate products.**
Go to **Settings → Data Cleanup → Consolidate Duplicates**. It keeps the newest copy and merges
everything onto it. Nothing is lost.

**A website won't import.**
Use **Open in Browser & Capture** instead of pasting the link, or add the product manually.

**I can't edit anything.**
You may be a Viewer, your studio may have run out of editor places, or its subscription may have
ended. Ask your studio owner.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?**

## 15. Your account

- **Display name:** set it in **Settings**. Teammates see it instead of your email address.
- **Sign out:** in **Settings** or the **More** menu. Your data stays in your studio.
- **Delete your account:** go to **Settings → Delete Account…**. The app walks you through
  transferring or deleting any studios you own. Deleting your account is permanent. It does not
  cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that separately in your Apple Account settings.
